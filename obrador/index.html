This catalog documents a selected body of original work.

Spanning physical assemblage, scale dioramas, hand-woven textiles, mixed-media collage, and a digital timepiece, the collection reflects a continuous investigation into material history, structural grid design, and system-based logic.

Rather than relying on a strict narrative, the practice centers on the objective recontextualization of physical and visual artifacts by transforming reclaimed boards, vintage trade tools, architectural hardware, upcycled textiles, and digital code into structured compositions.

Unified by an emphasis on patina, spatial geometry, and tactile contrast, these works record an evolving studio methodology grounded in physical assembly, scale manipulation, and mechanical precision.

HAND WOVEN PANELS

TEXTILE PANELS CONSTRUCTED FROM RECLAIMED COTTON, OXFORD, AND FINE-LINE PLAID SHIRTING STRIPS.

Executed on a custom pin-frame loom. The 8" x 6" composition compresses discarded fabrics into a dense, tactile grid that explores domestic reuse, material history, and structural tension.
